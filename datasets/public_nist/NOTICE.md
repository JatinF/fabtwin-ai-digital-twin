# Notice for the bundled NIST files

`README.txt` and `metrics_sets.zip` are unmodified copies of the NIST data publication accessed on 2026-10-21.

Bajcsy, Peter, Sathe, Pushkar, Vladar, Andras (2025), Detection Limits for SEM Image Segmentation, Version 1.0.0, National Institute of Standards and Technology, https://doi.org/10.18434/mds2-3838 (Accessed: 2026-10-21).

The NIST statement of copyright, fair use, and licensing is in `README.txt` and at https://www.nist.gov/director/copyright-fair-use-and-licensing-statements-srd-data-and-software

FabTwin did not modify those two files. `intensity_sets.zip` and `AImodel_sets.zip` were not downloaded. FabTwin did not run the U-Net models described in the archive. The SEM images in that publication are ARTIMAGEN simulations. They are public research imagery, not live production-fab data.
